SECTION 1: OVERVIEW OF NEET UG
NEET-UG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Undergraduate) is the single uniform entrance examination conducted by NTA for admission to undergraduate medical programs in India.
It covers admissions into MBBS, BDS (Dental), BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), BUMS (Unani), BSMS (Siddha), BVSc & AH (Veterinary Sciences), and B.Sc Nursing in all medical colleges including AIIMS and JIPMER.

SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR NEET UG
1. Subjects Required: Passed Class 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as core subjects.
2. Aggregate Marks: Minimum 50% aggregate marks in PCB for General category, 40% for SC/ST/OBC, and 45% for General-PwD.
3. Age Limit: Candidate must complete 17 years of age on or before 31st December of the year of admission. There is NO upper age limit.
4. Nationality: Indian Nationals, NRIs, OCIs, PIOs, and Foreign Nationals are eligible.

SECTION 3: EXAM STRUCTURE & SYLLABUS
- Total Marks: 720 Marks.
- Total Questions: 200 Questions (Section A: 35 compulsory, Section B: 15 questions with choice to attempt 10).
- Subject Breakup:
  * Physics: 45 Questions (180 Marks)
  * Chemistry: 45 Questions (180 Marks)
  * Biology (Botany & Zoology): 90 Questions (360 Marks)
- Marking Scheme: +4 marks for correct answer, -1 mark for incorrect answer.
- Exam Duration: 3 Hours 20 Minutes (Pen-Paper OMR Sheet Based).

SECTION 4: COUNSELING & SEAT ALLOCATION
1. 15% All India Quota (AIQ) Seats: Conducted by Medical Counseling Committee (MCC) for government colleges across India, AIIMS, JIPMER, and Deemed Universities.
2. 85% State Quota Seats: Conducted by respective State Counseling Authorities for state domicile candidates.

SECTION 5: ALLIED HEALTH & ALTERNATIVE MEDICAL CAREERS
If a student does not get MBBS, top high-scope alternatives include:
- BDS (Dental Surgery): 5 years including 1-year internship. High clinical practice potential.
- B.Sc Nursing & B.Pharm (Pharmacy): 4-year degree programs with immense employment opportunities globally.
- BPT (Physiotherapy) & BOT (Occupational Therapy): High demand in sports medicine and rehabilitation.
- B.Sc Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) & Radiology.
- Biotechnology / Bioinformatics / Genetics Research.
